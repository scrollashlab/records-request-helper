# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Plans a request for a veteran's Official Military Personnel File: who may ask, the fee, archival status, 1973 fire risk, what to gather, where to send it, and a ready-to-sign cover letter.
- Runs in the browser. Nothing a visitor types leaves the page.
- Pipeline covers verify, secure, configure, govern, package, deploy, monitor and release, and reports its Software Carbon Intensity on every run.
- Two GitLab Duo flows, Issue-to-MR and Pipeline Medic, take an issue to production without a person stepping in.
